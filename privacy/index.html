Privacy Policy

crowsvscrops is a personal upload tool for the developer's own TikTok account.

It stores only the TikTok access token for that account, locally on the developer's computer, to upload the developer's own videos to the account's drafts. It reads basic profile info (user.info.basic) only to confirm which account is connected.

No data from other users is collected, stored, sold or shared. The token can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings.
